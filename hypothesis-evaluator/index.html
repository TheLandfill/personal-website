Hypothesis Plausibility

Given each of the possible worlds specified by the constraints being true or false, assign a credibility to each hypothesis. To enforce the probabilities must add to 100% rule, the null-ish hypothesis will be set to 100% - (the sum of all other probabilities). The null-ish probability will be clamped to be between 5% and 95%, so other probabilities will be clamped so that the total never goes above 100%.
